# Embryo Fast Captions

Embryo Fast Captions is Embryo-Zero's fork of [faster-whisper](https://github.com/SYSTRAN/faster-whisper) (v1.2.1-19-g6cb639e, commit 6cb639e). It does fast speech to text.

- Our changes live on the `embryo` branch. The original license files (LICENSE) are unchanged.
- Legal notes: LEGAL.md. Dated list of our changes: CHANGES-EMBRYO.md.
